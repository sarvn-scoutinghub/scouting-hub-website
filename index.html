    Direct Placement & International Recruiting

    Finding the right talent for your company

    Searching for qualified employees can be time-consuming, especially when suitable professionals are sought internationally.

    Scouting Hub supports companies with structured candidate searches and guides the recruiting process from needs assessment to preparing the start of employment.

    Our Services

    Needs analysis

    Identifying the required qualifications, experience and requirements.

    International candidate search

    Identifying potentially suitable candidates through available networks.

    Pre-selection and qualification check

    Review of relevant details and existing proof of qualifications.

    Interview coordination

    Organising and coordinating job interviews between companies and candidates.

    Support with the hiring process

    Assistance with organisational procedures and required documents.

    Support with the start in Germany

    Depending on the agreed scope of services, coordination of further organisational steps.

    Our recruiting process

    1

    Analysis of your staffing needs

    2

    Search for suitable candidates

    3

    Pre-selection and coordination of interviews

    4

    Support in the selection and hiring process

    5

    Coordination of the agreed organisational steps before the start of work

    Our goal is to bring companies and suitable talent together efficiently and to enable a structured recruiting process.
